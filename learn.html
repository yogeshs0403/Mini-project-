CYBERSECURITY BASICS

Knowledge is your first defence.

Simple steps that help protect your information and accounts.

✉

1. Phishing awareness

Phishing messages try to trick people into sharing information or opening unsafe links. Watch for unexpected urgency, unusual sender addresses, spelling mistakes and requests for sensitive details. Visit important websites by typing their address yourself.

▣

2. Strong passwords

Use a long, unique password or passphrase for each account. Avoid personal details and reused passwords. A reputable password manager can help you create and store unique passwords.

◎

3. Multi-factor authentication

Turn on MFA wherever it is available. It adds another verification step, so a password alone is less likely to be enough to access your account. Never share one-time verification codes.

⌁

4. Safe browsing and updates

Keep your operating system, browser and apps updated. Download software from trusted sources, check website addresses and avoid entering sensitive information on suspicious pages.

◈

5. Public Wi-Fi safety

Be cautious on public networks. Prefer trusted websites using HTTPS, avoid sensitive transactions on networks you do not trust, and disable automatic connection to unknown Wi-Fi networks.
